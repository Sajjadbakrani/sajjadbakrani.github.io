I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences (ICMC), University of São Paulo, working with Prof. Tiago Pereira. My research focuses primarily on dynamical systems, with interests in both theoretical and applied problems. My publications are listed here.

Before joining ICMC, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Kadir Has University, working with Dr. Deniz Eroglu. I received my PhD in Mathematics from Imperial College London, under the supervision of Prof. Dmitry Turaev and Prof. Jeroen S. W. Lamb.

Research interests

  • Homoclinic dynamics and bifurcations
  • Networked dynamical systems
  • Reconstruction of dynamical systems from data
