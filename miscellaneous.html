Reading group on analysis and related topics

For the past 30 years, USP has had a tradition of informal meetings on analysis and related topics, open to anyone interested, including students of mathematics, physics and engineering, as well as high school students. The meetings are currently coordinated by Eddie Nijholt and me, and take place at ICMC every Saturday morning. If you’re interested in joining us, feel free to drop me a message!
